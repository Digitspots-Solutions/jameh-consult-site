About Us

Jameh Consult is a forward-thinking fintech company headquartered in Karu, Nasarawa State, Nigeria. We specialize in providing cutting-edge financial technology solutions that empower businesses and individuals to thrive in the digital economy. Our expertise spans payment gateway integration, financial software development, digital banking solutions, and fintech consulting services. With deep understanding of the Nigerian financial landscape and regulatory environment, we deliver solutions that are compliant, secure, and tailored to local market needs.
